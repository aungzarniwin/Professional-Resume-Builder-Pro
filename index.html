Interview Day Checklist

STAR Method

S — Situation: Describe the business situation.

T — Task: Explain your responsibility.

A — Action: Describe what you personally did.

R — Result: Explain the actual outcome, using verified numbers where available.

Tip: Prepare real examples of target achievement, problem solving, team coaching, customer management, and conflict resolution.
